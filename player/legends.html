What's in the pack

Five worlds, each a level theme in three layers that build with the action: explore, then action, then chase. Briar Keep is fairy-tale 6/8 with harp, pizzicato, bassoon and whistling. Toadstool Bayou is a swampy jug band with banjo, jaw harp, tuba, washboard and kazoo. Fiesta Calavera is a day-of-the-dead mariachi with guitarrón, accordion and trumpets in thirds. Lagoon Agents is undersea spy surf with vibes, walking bass, surf guitar, flute and muted brass. Thunder Olympus is mythic, with choir, harp, timpani, horns and a trumpet fanfare.

Glims are the collectibles. Each pickup plays the next tone of the current chord, so a row of them plays a melody. King glims turn them golden, with octave shimmer and a tiny "ya!". Each world also has stingers in its own key: checkpoint, freed (the little creatures cheer), king, death and level complete.

Kazoo Rumble is a musical level in the Rayman Legends tradition. It's a 48-bar gypsy-polka romp at 132 BPM whose gameplay is written into the music. Every jump lands on a brass "ba!" and a cymbal, every punch on a snare-and-clap hit, every slide on a swell, and the glims play the tune. The action lane is exported as a beatmap, so a level designer places obstacles on beats.

Everything is synthesized by Canopy Orchestra, with no samples and no quoted melodies. Render the pack with cd src && python3 -m tracks.legends --web; the engine pack, with OGG files, the beatmap and legends.json, goes to out/legends/. API: CanopyGame.setWorld(id), setLayer(1–3), collect(), king(on), sting(name), startLevel({auto}), input('jump'|'punch'|'slide'), getState(), on('bar'|'action'|'judgement'|'section', fn).
