About

The kapok is the tallest tree in the rainforest. KAPOK climbs it, from the roots to the stars where the game's space half begins. It moves through a 15/8 ostinato, a creature hocket, a 3/4 rain pastoral, an Amarok-style roll call of every instrument, an Ommadawn-style drum ensemble, a procession of instruments introduced one by one ("…plus tubular bells"), and a reel that keeps speeding up.

Every instrument is synthesized by Canopy Orchestra (src/orchestra), with no samples or recordings. The optional announcer is text-to-speech and is mixed in live here. Render the suite with cd src && python3 -m tracks.kapok, then export it for this page with python3 -m tracks.kapok_web ../out/kapok.

Same API as the adaptive player: CanopyGame.on('bar'|'movement', fn), CanopyGame.event('collect'), CanopyGame.seek(seconds), CanopyGame.getState().
